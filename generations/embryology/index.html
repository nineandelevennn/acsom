Master Embryogenesis Timeline Cheat-Sheet

Day-by-Day Cellular Roles, Rule of 2s/3s & High-Yield USMLE Associations

Week 2 = "The Week of Twos" (Days 8–14)
  • 2 Germ Layers (Bilaminar): Epiblast + Hypoblast
  • 2 Cavities: Amniotic cavity + Primary yolk sac
  • 2 Trophoblast layers: Cytotrophoblast + Syncytiotrophoblast
  • 2 Extraembryonic mesoderms: Somatic + Splanchnic
Week 3 = "The Week of Threes" (Days 15–21)
  • Gastrulation: Primitive streak forms on Day 14–15
  • Epiblast invaginates to form all 3 Germ Layers (Ectoderm, Mesoderm, Endoderm)
  • Notochord induces overlying ectoderm → Neural Plate
Day 0
Fertilization: Secondary oocyte arrested in Metaphase II is fertilized in the ampulla of the Fallopian tube. Cortical reaction blocks polyspermy.
Days 1–3
Cleavage: Zygote undergoes rapid mitotic divisions inside the intact zona pellucida while swept toward the uterus by tubal cilia.
Days 3–4
Morula (16–32 cells): Solid ball of blastomeres ("mulberry") enters the uterine cavity. Compaction segregates inner vs. outer cells.
Day 5
Blastocyst Formation & Hatching: Fluid-filled blastocoel cavity forms. Zona pellucida degenerates so the blastocyst can implant. Inner Cell Mass (embryoblast) + Outer Trophoblast.
Days 6–7
Implantation: Embryonic pole attaches to posterior superior uterine wall. Trophoblast differentiates into Cytotrophoblast (mitotic cells) & Syncytiotrophoblast (invades endometrium, secretes β-hCG).
Days 7–8 (Trap!)
Bilaminar Disc Forms: Inner cell mass splits into Epiblast (dorsal columnar) & Hypoblast (ventral cuboidal). Small clefts coalesce inside the Epiblast to form the Amniotic Cavity (amnioblasts), while Hypoblast cells migrate to line the Primary Yolk Sac (Heuser membrane).
Days 9–12
Lacunar Stage & Uteroplacental Circulation: Syncytiotrophoblast lacunae fuse with maternal sinusoids. β-hCG becomes detectable in maternal serum (~Day 8–10) and urine (~Day 14).
Days 14–15 (Wk 3)
Gastrulation Begins: Epiblast forms the Primitive Streak and invaginates to give rise to ALL THREE germ layers: Endoderm (replaces hypoblast), Mesoderm, and Ectoderm.
Days 16–21
Neurulation Begins: Notochord (from primitive node) secretes Sonic Hedgehog (SHH) to induce overlying ectoderm into the Neural Plate. Neural folds elevate.
Week 4 (Days 22–28)
Heart Beats & Neural Tube Closes: Heart begins beating (Day 22). Anterior neuropore closes (Day 25 → failure = anencephaly); Posterior neuropore closes (Day 27 → failure = spina bifida). Upper/lower limb buds appear.
